Colors, type and motion

Toolcrib is built like a precision-cut foam insert: the page is the top layer of foam and every product sits in its own pocket cut down to a colored floor. All of it is set in Theme settings.

#Colors

  • Background and Text for the page, the header shell and raised surfaces.
  • Pocket floor: the color inside every pocket. Keep it light when product photos have white backgrounds.
  • Accent: buttons, links, the quick add notch and focus rings.
  • In stock, Low stock and Sale and errors for status colors.

Toolcrib checks nothing for you here, so keep text on backgrounds at a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1. The three presets already meet it.

#Type

Pick Headings, sizes and prices and Body fonts from Shopify's font library, and the Body text size. Sizes and prices use the heading font, so choose one with clear numerals.

#Layout

SettingWhat it does
Page widthThe widest the content gets on large screens.
CornersRounder or squarer pockets, buttons and fields.
MotionFull, subtle or off. Visitors who ask their device for less motion always get subtle motion.
Reveal sections on scrollSections rise in gently as they come into view.
Section heading alignmentLeft or centered section headings.
Add a faint foam grain to the pageA very light texture on the page background.

#Headings with an accent word

Every section heading accepts word to set one word in the accent color: Find the exact part.

#Product cards

Theme settingsProduct cards controls what every card shows: SKU, stock, pack, vendor, a second image on hover, quick add, quick view, quantity, compare, save to list, badges and the image shape.

Cut pockets to the product outline makes each pocket follow the shape of the product. Use it only when your product images have transparent backgrounds (PNG or WebP); out-of-stock products then show as an empty pocket.

Tip

For the cleanest grid, photograph every product from the same angle and light, and give each square image the same margin.
