Help

Questions and troubleshooting

#Product data

#The size does not show on product cards

The card shows caliper.size. Check that the definition exists in SettingsCustom dataProducts with exactly that namespace and key, and that the product has a value. If your sizes are variant options instead, turn on Theme settingsProduct cardsUse variant options as the size.

#Specifications show as one line

Write one specification per line with a colon between label and value, in a Multi-line text field. A single line text field cannot hold line breaks.

#Pockets are rectangles, not product shapes

Cut pockets to the product outline needs product images with transparent backgrounds (PNG or WebP). With white backgrounds, leave it off.

#Search and filters

#Can customers search by part number?

Yes. Suggestions match variant SKUs and barcodes and open the matching variant. Set Theme settingsSearchExample part number so buyers know they can.

#Drive or head type filters show as a checklist

Add caliper.drive or caliper.head as a filter in Search & Discovery. Any filter whose name contains "drive" or "head" (also in French, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese) shows as drawings.

#A filter I added does not appear

Filters show only when products in the collection have values for them. Check a product in that collection, then reload the collection.

#Navigation

#Where is the Order pad link?

It appears once a page with the handle order-pad exists, or when you choose a page in Header > Order pad page. Turn it off with Show order pad link.

#The dropdown shows no columns

Nest menu items under a top-level item in ContentMenus. Only nested items open a dropdown.

#Cart and checkout

#Checkout is greyed out

Ask customers to agree to terms before checkout is on. The buyer must tick the box; it is remembered for the visit.

#Express checkout buttons are missing in the drawer

Turn on Show express checkout buttons in the Cart drawer section, and check that Shop Pay, Apple Pay or Google Pay is active in SettingsPayments.

#Pop-ups

#The pop-up does not open while I test

It opens once per visit, after the delay counted from your first tap, click or key press, and stays hidden for the days set after you close it. Test in a private window. In the theme editor it opens when you select its section.

#B2B

#Do I need an app for B2B pricing?

No. Shopify B2B catalogs supply prices and quantity rules; Caliper shows them. Retail buyers see regular prices.

#Reorder shows "Sign in"

Reorder lists a customer's own orders, so it needs a signed-in customer. Customer accounts must be on in SettingsCustomer accounts.

#Updates and apps

#Will updates overwrite my changes?

Settings and content carry over, and setting names never change between versions. Code you edit yourself is not carried over, so prefer settings, custom Liquid blocks and app blocks.

#How do I add a review app?

Install it, then add its app blocks in the theme editor. Tested with Judge.me (free plan) on 2026-11-24:

  • Star rating under the title: Caliper already shows stars on the product page and on cards from the rating your review app saves to the product (Shopify's standard rating metafield; Judge.me fills it). If you also add the app's own star badge to Product information, turn off Size and title › Show star rating so the stars show once.
  • Review list: add a Custom section below Product information and put the app's review widget in it, so it runs the full width of the page. It can also go inside Product information or Featured product.
  • Dark preset (Gauge) or dark mode: review apps set their own text colors, often black. In the review widget's settings, set the text color to a light gray (for example #EDEEEF) and the lighter text to #9BA1A8, or the reviews are hard to read on the dark background.
  • Speed: Judge.me adds about 350 KB of script to the product page; Lighthouse performance stayed at 94–96 on mobile in the test.
