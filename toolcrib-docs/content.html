Sample content

The sample content pack has what the Toolcrib demos are built from. Import it into a new or development store to see every feature with real-looking data, then replace it with your catalog.

#What is in the pack

FileContents
caliper-hardware-products.csvAbout 40 hardware and tool products with sizes, packs, specifications and pictures (Toolcrib preset).
gauge-auto-products.csvAbout 40 auto parts with vehicle compatibility lists (Redline preset).
foundry-industrial-products.csvAbout 40 industrial supply products with trade packs (Anvil preset).
metafield-definitions.jsonEvery product, variant and collection field Toolcrib reads, with its type.

Download the sample content pack (ZIP)

Note

Product names, part numbers, prices and stock levels are invented for the demos. Vehicle names in compatibility lists only show how the feature works; they are not fitment data.

#Import it

  1. Create the metafield definitions first. See Product data.
  2. Open ProductsImport, choose one CSV and select Upload and preview, then Import products.
  3. Pictures load from the links in the file. Large imports take a few minutes; Shopify emails you when they finish.

#Remove it later

Open Products, filter by Vendor (Northgate, Redcourse or Halvard), select all and choose Delete products.
