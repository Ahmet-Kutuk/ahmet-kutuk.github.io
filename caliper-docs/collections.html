Pages

Collection pages

Collection pages use the Product grid section. Open a collection in the theme editor to set it up.

#Views

Buyers switch between three views with the buttons above the grid:

  • Grid: product cards with size, picture, name, SKU, stock and price.
  • List: one row per product with more detail.
  • Table: size, stock, price and a quantity field on every row, for buyers who order many parts at once. The column headings sort the page by product, size, stock or price.

Density (Theme settingsLayoutDensity) set to Compact tightens grids and tables for long trade catalogs, and Product grid on phones chooses one or two cards per row.

When filters leave no products, the page lists each active filter so the buyer can drop the one that is too narrow.

With filters applied, Save this search keeps the filtered view in the buyer's browser; saved views appear when the search field is focused and empty.

Card style (Theme settingsProduct cardsCard style): Standard, Compact (size, name, part number, stock and price only) or Feature (a taller photo and a larger name).

The finder collection template puts the part finder and filters above a wide grid with "Load more"; assign it to large categories. The collections list shows an A to Z index when it has more than 12 categories.

#Banner and subcategories

  • Show collection image puts the collection image beside the title. Banner video, or a caliper.banner_video file on the collection, plays a short silent loop instead, with a pause button.
  • Show subcategories shows tiles for the collections listed under this collection in the menu, plus a "Shop all" tile.

#Pagination

ChoiceBehavior
PagesNumbered pages. Best for very large catalogs and sharing links to a page.
Load moreA button adds the next products below.
Infinite scrollThe next products load as the buyer nears the end.

#Promotion tiles

Add Promotion tile blocks to place a picture card in the grid at the position you choose, for an offer or a guide.

#Cards

What each card shows is set once for the whole store in Theme settingsProduct cards: SKU, stock, a stock bar, pack, vendor, a second image on hover, quick add, quick view, quantity, compare and save to list.

Badges appear on their own: sold out, the percentage saved, New (for the days set in Show "New" for), low stock, and any product tag written as badge:Text, for example badge:Best seller.

#Compare

With Show compare on, a Compare switch appears above the grid. When it is on, each card gets a compare box; buyers pick up to four products and open a side-by-side table with live price, stock and every specification lined up by label. Show differences only hides matching rows.
