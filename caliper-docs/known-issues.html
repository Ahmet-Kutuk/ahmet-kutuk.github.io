Known issues

These are the limits we know about in Caliper 1.0.0. Each one says why it happens and what you can do. Fixed issues move to the changelog.

#Prices for trade customers only: Shopify still lists prices in its page data

With Theme settings › Trade › Prices for logged-in customers on, the theme hides every price and add-to-cart button from guests. Shopify's own analytics script (ShopifyAnalytics.meta) still carries product prices in the page source, and the theme cannot remove it. If prices must stay private, use Shopify B2B catalogs, which keep prices out of the storefront for guests altogether.

#The second photo on hover needs a mouse

The card's second photo shows on hover. Phones and keyboards have no hover, so they keep the first photo; the second is always in the product page gallery.

#Prefetching works in Chromium browsers

Product and collection links are fetched in advance as the pointer settles on them (Speculation Rules). Safari and Firefox do not support this yet and load pages the usual way.

#Brands in the mega menu need a menu with sub-items

The brand row at the bottom of a mega menu lists the vendors of the category's collection. It appears only for menu items that open a mega menu, which needs sub-items under the item.

#Replaced part numbers are not searchable on their own

The "Replaces …" note in search reads caliper.alt_part_numbers. Shopify's search does not index that metafield, so add the old numbers as product tags, or make the metafield searchable in the Search & Discovery app.

#The millimetre and inch switch converts lengths only

The switch on the specification table converts values written with mm, in or ″. Pipe and thread sizes (1/2″ NPT, 1/4-20 UNC) are names, not lengths, and are left as written. Screw gauges (#8) are not converted.

#The dispatch promise uses the store's time zone at page load

The "Order within …" countdown works from the store's time zone offset when the page is loaded. On the night clocks change, a page left open across the change can be an hour off until it is reloaded.
