#Trade

For stores that sell to trade customers. With Shopify B2B, company catalogs set prices on their own; these settings are for stores without it.

SettingTypeDefaultWhat it does
Show prices to signed-in customers onlyOn / offOffOther shoppers see "Log in to see prices" in place of prices and add to cart buttons. The theme leaves prices out of its pages, but Shopify's own analytics data on the page still carries them; for prices that must stay private, use Shopify B2B catalogs.
Customer tagTextOptional. Only signed-in customers with this tag see prices, for example: trade.
