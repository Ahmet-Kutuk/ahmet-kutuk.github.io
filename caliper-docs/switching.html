Switching from another theme

Caliper reads the same Shopify data every theme reads, so products, collections, menus and customers carry over untouched. What changes is where the extra detail lives. This guide moves it in the order that matters most.

#1. Install without publishing

Add Caliper to your theme library and open it in the editor. Your current theme stays live until you publish.

#2. Part numbers

Caliper's search matches SKUs and barcodes, and ignores dashes and spaces. Check that each variant has its part number in the SKU field. If your old theme or an app kept part numbers in a metafield instead, copy them into SKU with a bulk edit or a product CSV.

#3. Specifications

Caliper reads one multi-line metafield, caliper.specs, with one Label: value per line. If your specifications sit in several metafields today, export products to CSV, join the columns into that format, and import. Product data has the definitions and a CSV example.

#4. Sizes, packs and stock wording

Fill caliper.size (the large size on cards and the product page) and caliper.pack ("Box of 100"). Cards and the product page use them straight away.

#5. Filters

Filters come from the Search & Discovery app, not from the theme, so the ones you have keep working. Caliper adds pictures for drive and head types and a finder above the grid; see Filters.

#6. Menus

Menus carry over. For mega menus with pictures, give each top item sub-items, and set a category image and color code on the collections it links to (Navigation).

#7. Apps

App blocks work in the product page, featured product, custom section, footer and cart. Apps that edited your old theme's code need to be added again; check with the app's support.

#8. Before you publish

Preview the home page, a collection, a product with options, the cart and search on a phone and a computer. Then publish.
