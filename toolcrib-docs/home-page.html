Pages

Home page sections

Add sections with Add section in the theme editor and drag them into order. Every section with a heading accepts word for an accent word and has a Band setting (none, dark, accent or raised) to set it apart.

#Part search

The home page search bar for buyers who know what they want.

  1. Add Example search blocks with real searches, such as a part number, a size and a product type. Buyers tap them to see how search works.
  2. Add up to four Service fact blocks: same-day dispatch, trade accounts, counter pickup.
  3. With Layout > Search and tray over a photo and a Background photo, the search and a tray of parts sit over a wide workshop photo.

#Category drawers

One block per collection, each with its color, product count and a picture (the collection image, or its first product). Good as the second section, right under the search.

#Sections for products

SectionUse it for
Featured collectionA row or grid of products from one collection.
Collection tabsSeveral collections behind tabs, such as "New", "Best sellers", "On sale".
DealsProducts with a countdown to an end date you set. Only use a real end date.
Featured productOne product with its options and buy button.
Image hotspotsDots on a photo that open product cards, for "shop the look" of a workbench or a kit.
Recently viewedThe buyer's last viewed products.

#Sections for content

SectionUse it for
SlideshowUp to several slides with a heading, text and button. Autoplay starts once the buyer uses the page, with pause and swipe.
VideoA YouTube, Vimeo or uploaded video, loaded only when played. An uploaded video can instead Play muted on a loop, with pause and sound buttons.
Shop by jobCards for jobs ("Deck build", "Bathroom refit") linking to the collection or guide that holds what each needs, with the part count.
Project galleryFinished jobs with the parts used in each.
HotspotsA photo with pins on the parts in it; each pin opens the part with its price.
Before and afterTwo photos of the same scene with a handle to compare, such as rust removal.
StatsA few numbers that count up. Use only true figures.
FAQQuestions and answers, with structured data for search engines.
Brands we stockLogos in a grid or a scrolling strip, linked to each brand's products.
Collection carouselRound or square category pictures in a row.
Contact and mapAddress, hours, phone and a directions link.
Rich text, Newsletter, Custom LiquidText, an email signup and your own code.
Setup checklistShown only in the theme editor: ticks off logo, menu, products, collections, part numbers and policies as you set them up.

Some sections come in two versions in Add section, such as the part search over a photo, a featured collection on a dark band and a statement on a dark band.

Tip

Phones show rows of cards sideways instead of stacking them, so a long home page stays short on a small screen.

#Vehicle finder (Redline)

Add one Vehicle block per vehicle with its make, model and years. Buyers choose year, make and model; the results show parts whose caliper.fitment list matches. Chosen vehicles are kept as My garage in the header when Header > Saved items is set to garage.
